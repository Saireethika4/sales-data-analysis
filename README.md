# AN ANALYTICAL STUDY OF RETAIL SALES DATA FOR DECISION SUPPORT SYSTEMS

--> Abstract
Retail analytics is currently undergoing a massive transformation, moving away from the rigid, "after-the-fact" reporting of the past toward interactive systems that help managers make choices in real-time.
Today's digital economy generates more data than most organizations can properly handle, making data-driven decision-making (DDDM) less of a luxury and more of a survival tactic for staying resilient in a high-velocity market. This research develops a "Customer-Centric Market Basket Analysis" (CC-MBA) framework to address the computational inefficiencies and limitations of older, product-focused models. By applying K-means clustering to Recency, Frequency, and Monetary (RFM) metrics, we are able to segment customers based on actual behaviour, identifying high-value groups that significantly impact a store's bottom line. To move beyond just describing what happened yesterday, we implemented Gradient Boosting forecasting models, which captured complex, non-linear patterns far more effectively than traditional linear regression methods.

--> KEY WORDS: Machine Learning, Sales, Sales Forecasting, Deep Learning, Retail Sales.
